Watten – Card Game

The card game with the Bavarian deck for Android: four players in two pairs, the Schlag and the trump are called, and whoever wins three of five tricks wins the round. Play the Bavarian or the Tyrolean way and adjust the rules in detail.

What the app does

  • Four seats in two pairs: alone with computer partners, taking turns with several people on one device, or online against others.
  • Adjustable rules: Bavarian or Tyrolean style, target points, Kritische, Guate, Weli, trump duty, raising the stake, starting dealer and time per turn.
  • Four computer levels: Beginner, Medium, Hard and Expert.
  • With several people on one device, the hand is hidden after every turn.
  • Tap cards to play them. Easier-to-distinguish colours for colour blindness, table colours, sounds you can switch on and off one by one, German and English.
  • Your offline game is saved, and statistics stay on the device.

Offline without an account, online with an email address

The modes without a network need neither an account nor the internet. The online mode is optional and requires a confirmed email address; details are in the privacy policy.
