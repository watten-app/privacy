Delete account

For the online account of the Android app Watten (package ID: rp.watten).

In the app

  1. Open Watten and choose “Play online”.
  2. In the “Account” section tap “Delete account” and confirm the prompt.

The account, the email address, the push identifiers, the premium status with the check value of the purchase token, your partners, invitations, block list, overall record and the link to your games are deleted immediately. An existing premium subscription is not cancelled by this; you do that in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions). Running games are forfeited: a computer takes over your seat and the game continues. The app also removes the account data from your device.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app or cannot open it, write from the email address you registered with to FsNaviVfr@gmail.com and ask for your account to be deleted. I normally delete the account and the address within 30 days. If the mail comes from another address, I will ask a follow-up question.

What is deleted

Email address, account ID, check values of the device keys, player name, push identifiers, premium status with the check value of the purchase token, partners, invitations, block list, overall record (also in other people’s lists) and the link to games. Reports by others about you remain until 90 days are up; a ban stores only the check value of the address. Game states of finished games that no longer link to an account are deleted 30 days after the game ends. More in the privacy policy.
