Terms of use for the online mode

Last updated: 30 October 2026. These terms apply to the online mode of the Android app Watten. The modes without a network need no agreement.

1. Provider

Robert Peringer, Ittlinger Hauptstraße 17, 94315 Straubing, Germany, email: FsNaviVfr@gmail.com. Further details are in the legal notice.

2. Service

The online mode lets you play with other people on different devices. It can be used free of charge; in the free use up to five online games per calendar week (Monday to Sunday, UTC) can be started, after which no further online games can be started until the week changes (games against the computer and on the same device are not affected, and games in progress can be finished). A game counts as started as soon as all four seats are filled or the creator starts it with at most two computer seats; it counts for every participating person. With the optional premium subscription (section 3a) this limit does not apply to you. I aim for stable operation but cannot promise uninterrupted availability; maintenance, faults and changes can interrupt the service from time to time. I may change or discontinue the service for a factual reason and will announce this in the app or on this website beforehand where possible.

3. Access and account

  • The online mode is intended for people aged 18 and over.
  • The account belongs to your email address, which you confirm with a code. There is one account per address. You are responsible for the address being yours and for keeping access to your mailbox. If a device or the app data is lost, you sign in again with the address.
  • The player name is assigned at random at first; you can change it (3 to 20 characters, not starting with “KI”). It is shown to the other players in your games. An account that has not been used for a year is deleted.

3a. Premium subscription (optional)

  • What it covers: With the premium subscription the weekly limit from section 2 does not count for you. The limit of other people is not affected: if a host without premium has reached the limit, a game cannot start or be accepted. Further technical limits, for example on the number of games running at the same time, continue to apply. All other features are unchanged.
  • Purchase and price: The subscription is taken out and paid through Google Play (payment processing by Google). The app shows the term and price (final price including VAT) before purchase, and so does Google Play in the purchase dialog; the information shown there is authoritative.
  • Renewal and cancellation: The subscription renews automatically for the booked term until you cancel it. You can cancel at any time in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions); cancellation takes effect at the end of the period already paid, and premium stays usable until then. Deleting the online account or the app does not end the subscription; you still have to cancel it in Google Play. The same applies if an account is deleted after a year without use (section 3): premium ends with the account, while the subscription at Google continues until you cancel it.
  • Assignment: Premium applies to the online account with which the purchase was redeemed in the app, not to other accounts; a purchase can be assigned to one account only. The app compares purchases with the service when you open the online area; “Check purchases” triggers this. It can take a moment for premium to show on a device or account.
  • Withdrawal, refunds, faults: The terms of Google Play also apply to payment, price changes, refunds and any right of withdrawal; you request refunds there. Premium ends at the latest at the end of the paid period; if a purchase is refunded or revoked it may end earlier, as soon as the service learns of it (the app compares purchases only when you open the online area). Statutory rights you have as a consumer remain unaffected.
  • Availability: I aim for stable operation (section 2); uninterrupted availability of the online mode is not promised even with premium.

4. Fair use

Please use the service only as intended. In particular, the following are not allowed:

  • automated access, tampering with the app or the service, and attempts to see other players’ hands,
  • deliberately delaying games, and creating many accounts to get around limits,
  • actions that overload the service or affect others,
  • offensive, discriminatory or misleading display names, impersonating another person, and harassing other players.

In case of violations I may block or delete accounts and end games.

5. Further features

  • Partners and invitations: Anyone who joins a game with a code and the creator of that game are saved as partners of each other; with partners you can start games without a code. You can remove partners at any time.
  • Overall record: For players against whom you have finished games the app shows you the overall record (wins and losses against individual opponents). It has no meaning outside the app.
  • Turn clock: Whoever creates a game chooses a time per turn or none. If a time applies, the creator can decide whether it pauses while the player to move is not in the game, having left the app or the game (default), or keeps running. When the time runs out the service acts for the seat itself (it makes a call, plays a legal card or gives up); after three missed limits in a row a computer takes over the seat.
  • Notifications: Push messages (“Your turn”, invitation) exist only if you turn them on; they are off by default and can be turned off at any time.

6. Blocking, reporting and bans

You can block other players: you can then no longer invite each other or join each other’s games. You can also report them, with a fixed reason (display name, behaviour, other) and without free text. I review reports where possible but am not obliged to. In case of violations of section 4 I may ban an account; it is then deleted, and the email address is stored only as a check value so that it cannot sign up again. If you think a ban is unjustified, write to the address above; I will look into it and lift the ban where justified. I will tell you about a ban, with reasons, at the email address on file where possible and unless the rights of others or security reasons prevent it; I answer complaints within a reasonable time.

The online mode is not played for money or valuables, and there are no winnings.
